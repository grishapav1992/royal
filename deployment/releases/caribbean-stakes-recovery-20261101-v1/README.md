# Caribbean historical-stake recovery

A returning player with a completed rules-1 spin could reopen the pirate game with a new-spin selection of $1, $5, $10 or $20. The client multiplied the historical per-line stake by its old line count and reused that total as the new selected denomination. A fresh account already selected $0.10, so fresh-account checks missed this transition.

Package 1.6.2 selects the common ten-cent default after a completed historical round, while preserving the accepted stake and complete cost of an active historical round. Its continuations remain free. Current rules-2 stakes still restore normally. Prices show two decimal places, and an active historical series explains the next complete spin starts at $0.10. No probability, paytable, settlement or backend source changes.

The module projection preserves the exact published Caribbean inputs and media except CaribbeanUI.ts and package.json. Publish only these two source files and this compatible 1/2/1 package. Keep the host, backend, catalogue, other modules, settings, accounts and persistent volumes unchanged. The guarded publisher retains and verifies the old package and source archive.

Verification covers a real isolated online hall with chat on desktop and mobile, all four legacy line counts, actual ten-cent debits, replay/reopen without an extra debit, an accepted old bonus with fourteen free continuations and unchanged original cost, all nine current price choices, and restoration of an already confirmed current denomination. Production accounts, bets and private player data are not used.
