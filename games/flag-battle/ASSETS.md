# Flag assets

The original twelve flags remain local Royal SVG artwork. The 36 additional rectangular country flags are from [lipis/flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), commit `ac7e0eb3259eb62aa6100c435607e2fe03896a97`, directory `flags/4x3/`. They are distributed under the MIT license, preserved in `public/assets/flag-battle/flag-icons-LICENSE.txt`. No remote assets are fetched during gameplay.
