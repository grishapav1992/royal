# Forest Heart client 1.2

Independent client for `studio-forest-heart`, using Royal host API 1, rules 1 and progress 1. The common expansion transport and wallet own operations, sessions and money. This package only presents accepted public states. Mathematical rules: [rules-v1.md](../../docs/concepts/forest-heart/rules-v1.md).

## Event specification

The choices below are artistic and engineering hypotheses for this game, following [game-feedback.md](../../docs/game-feedback.md). Durations are not scientific perceptual thresholds. Every sound uses the host AudioContext and output.

| Event and trigger | Meaning and visual phases | Sound and synchronization | Interruption and verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Idle, visible and unpaused | Stable portrait, quiet fireflies and crystal aura; no whole-image rocking or reward | Synthesized plucked strings, breathy flute and warm pads; a sixteen-bar theme with rests, four phrases and three orchestration passes | Hidden, pause, mute, volume zero, close or account change cancels owned voices and room tails; reduced motion removes travel |
| A confirmed new field | Wood tiles open, then actual contacts become readable; no fake near miss | Dry low wood impact at the end of reveal | All results remain visible when decoration is skipped; never creates a request |
| Confirmed manual turn | Only the accepted root turns 90 degrees; new pulse follows its confirmed network | Short wooden click followed by bark friction at rotation completion | No entropy or fee from presentation; verify original collected flowers stay paid |
| Source pulse | Mint light travels through the actual powered connected component; branches remain readable | Water texture with separate droplets starts with the first lit root | A single frame clock uses the estimated audible output time; record timestamp mode, measured frame skew and latency separately |
| Connected flower | Petals open and show a series contribution; each receiver at most once per pulse | An inharmonic glass drop, three related receiver pitches | No final wallet-credit language; restore and repeated replies do not replay notes |
| Confirmed choice | Selectable roots become clear; the elf changes pose on the accepted turn | Leaf rustle and a falling two-note flute response | One cue per accepted transition; no reward classification |
| Confirmed bonus entry | Four bonus buds become clear | Low drum and gong body, swelling strings and one upper pluck; bonus music gains a separate percussion pattern | One cue per accepted transition; no special payout inferred from entering the bonus |
| Complete series | Exact total and net relative to the original paid stake | No celebration for zero, partial return or break-even; positive result gets a strummed string cadence; 10x adds a two-part low gong, drum and string response | The unchanged 10x threshold is this game's presentation choice. Skip cancels decoration only. Restored history stays silent |

Normal reveal and confirmed turn use a 460 ms preparation, scheduled to settle at the estimated audible wooden accent. Tiles arrive with a small stagger, 8 px maximum travel and smooth easing. A turn interpolates only the changed tile through 90 degrees; passing does not rotate tiles. The flowing artwork is revealed from its actual incoming port: the breadth-first wave advances 140 ms per mutual connection with a 210 ms local reveal. Fast mode uses 190 ms landing, 65 ms per connection and 100 ms reveal. These are art choices, not findings from the research. Reduced motion shows the confirmed state directly. Cue scheduling uses getOutputTimestamp when available, with outputLatency/baseLatency as a fallback. The engineering goal is at most 80 ms of visible frame skew from the estimated audible accent on the local test setup; this does not measure physical acoustic latency. Phone speakers, wired headphones, Bluetooth and human listening quality require separate listening records. Nonzero audio samples prove signal presence only.

Version 1.1 replaces the contour pipes with a painted wood atlas, an alpha root frame, a Cyrillic gold title and a crystal cradle. Its seven oriented shapes preserve the original masks. RootArtwork is display-only, exhaustively checked against an independent graph oracle. The elf uses a restrained 750 ms pose crossfade on the accepted choice; it is not a skeletal character animation. Original rules, scoring, qualified sources and wallet transport are unchanged. Asset prompts and hashes: [assets-v2.json](../../docs/concepts/forest-heart/assets-v2.json).

Version 1.2 gives events different materials, attacks and rhythms. Music uses a 76 BPM audio-clock schedule with a short look-ahead; delayed callbacks skip elapsed time instead of replaying old notes. Effects gently duck the music, including its room send. Turning music off only cancels music voices, preserving an accepted event's effect. All sample buffers and short stereo room responses are synthesized by the module, with a deterministic audio-only seed and no external recordings. The shared Royal context and preferences remain the only audio authority. [Audio design and comparison](../../docs/concepts/forest-heart/audio-v3-design.md) distinguish digital measurements from listening judgements.

RU/EN copy is local to the module. All controls are keyboard accessible. The choice is deliberate and has no timeout. There is no automatic paid spinning. Final verification and asset provenance are recorded under docs/concepts/forest-heart/.
