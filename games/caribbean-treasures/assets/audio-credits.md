# Recorded audio credits

Music: **Blackmoor Tides** by **Matthew Pablo** ([author](https://www.matthewpablo.com), [source release](https://opengameart.org/content/blackmoor-tides-epic-pirate-battle-theme)), [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Instrumental loop; lossless FLAC source transcode; two-pass loudness normalization, 30 ms music edge fades, stereo AAC export; runtime music ducking. Composition and duration preserved.

Source: Epic Pirate Battle Theme/Blackmoor Tides Loop (No Chants).wav. The lossless current master is audio-source/music.flac. The grant used is the explicitly CC-BY-3.0 release on OpenGameArt, not the author website’s general catalog. In-game rules include title, author, source, license and edit notice.

Effects: **Kenney**, Impact Sounds and RPG Audio ([Impact](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), [RPG](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio)), CC0. Included original pack license files are in audio-source/. **Thimras**, [Cannon hit wall](https://opengameart.org/content/cannon-hit-wall), CC0.

Exact source-to-cue mapping, changes and links are also shipped in public/assets/caribbean-treasures/audio/credits.json. Runtime music and all effects are samples; the player creates no oscillators.

Re-export with FFMPEG_PATH set to an installed FFmpeg binary: node scripts/export-caribbean-audio.mjs. Only selected current masters are retained, not whole downloaded libraries.
