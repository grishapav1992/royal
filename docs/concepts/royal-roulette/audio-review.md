## Design

Chip choice uses a short porcelain-like clink, alternating two similar takes. Table placement is lower and drier. The wheel and ball use restrained filtered-noise textures; speed and pan follow the existing visual pose. The landing plan schedules ten material contacts with diminishing taps and a final settling accent. A zero return receives a quiet neutral sound, a positive net a short warm chime, and a straight hit a fuller phrase. No reward cue plays on restored background results.

These timbres and levels are game-specific design hypotheses for a calm, repeatable sound. Original deterministic synthesis requires no downloaded recordings. Shared host output supplies master volume and limiting. The module creates no separate context or wallet. Muting, zero volume, hidden page, pause, account change, request failure and close cancel sources and future cues. Async unlock checks a lifetime generation so closed or interrupted work cannot start later.
