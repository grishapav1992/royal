# Flooded Temple audio

Original authored stereo PCM16, 24000 Hz, generated by `scripts/design/temple-audio.mjs`. These are local game resources, without downloaded samples, speech or third-party recordings. Rebuild with `node scripts/design/temple-audio.mjs` from the repository root. `games/flooded-temple/sounds.json` records durations, designed accent offsets, source peaks and RMS.

Files: `scrape`, `stone`, `step`, `chest`, `lever`, `rope`, `relic`, `neutral`, `return`, `win`, `big`, `ambience`, all `.wav`. `neutral` covers zero payouts and partial returns. `return`, `win` and `big` refer to the complete original paid stake, including free continuation. The audio owner never performs wallet operations.

The owner uses the common host AudioContext and output. It caps its live/releasing sources at ten, ducks its water loop for major events, fades canceled running voices over 12 ms and stops them after 13 ms. Those cancellation timings are engineering choices. Suspended contexts release immediately. Decode failures remain silent and retry on a later gesture; past cues are not queued.

The source waveforms have soft boundaries. Source peaks range from 0.04 to 0.30. Signal metrics and waveform checks do not assess subjective listening quality. Physical phone speakers, headphones and Bluetooth require a separate listening review.
